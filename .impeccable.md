## Design Context

### Users
- **Type**: Speculative traders and bot enthusiasts.
- **Context**: High-speed decision making, monitoring multiple metrics, seeking "edge" through automation.
- **Job**: Execute tick-based strategies (Martingale) with precision and emotional control.

### Brand Personality
- **Voice**: Technical, authoritative, focused.
- **Tone**: **Industrial Noir / Cyber-Terminal**.
- **3 Words**: Precision, Kinetic, Stealth.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Style**: Dark-mode primary, high-contrast "Amber" or "Mint" accents, monospace utility.
- **References**: Bloomberg Terminal (modernized), high-end mechanical keyboards, industrial control systems.
- **Anti-references**: Soft gradients, bubbly buttons, generic SaaS marketing icons.

### Design Principles
1. **Density with Clarity**: Information should be dense but easy to scan. No wasted white space.
2. **Kinetic Feedback**: Every trade or tick should have subtle, crisp motion (no bounce, just fast transitions).
3. **Typography as Structure**: Use JetBrains Mono for all data; Space Grotesk for bold headers.
4. **Intentional Decoration**: Use subtle grid lines and scan-line patterns to reinforce the "Terminal" feel.
5. **Color-Coded Status**: Greens and Reds (Up/Down) must be distinct but not overwhelming (OKLCH for vibrance).
